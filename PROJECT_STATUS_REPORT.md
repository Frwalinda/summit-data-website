## Executive Summary

The Summit Data Ltd website has been rebuilt around the company's current positioning: a practical digital transformation and implementation partner for field-based business systems across African markets.

The new site is designed to feel simple, credible, corporate, mobile-friendly, and operationally grounded. It presents Summit Data as more than a software or website company, with emphasis on Sales Force Automation, QR/GPS asset tracking, cooler visibility, geo-mapping, field data collection, dashboards, workflow automation, and hands-on rollout support.

The strongest credibility anchor is now the SBC Kenya / PepsiCo Kenya Sales Force Automation rollout, including cooler tracking, QR tagging, GPS verification, distributor and ASR onboarding, daily reporting visibility, Nairobi rollout, Central/Mountain region onboarding, Coastal region planned next, and ongoing stabilisation/enhancement.

## Current Site Structure

The site is generated by a custom Node build script. Source content and page templates live in `src/` and `scripts/`, while generated static output lives in `dist/`.

Primary generated pages:

- `dist/index.html`
- `dist/solutions.html`
- `dist/case-studies.html`
- `dist/industries.html`
- `dist/about.html`
- `dist/contact.html`

Legacy redirects are also generated for older paths:

- `dist/services.html` redirects to `solutions.html`
- `dist/projects.html` redirects to `case-studies.html`

Generated supporting files:

- `dist/assets/styles.css`
- `dist/assets/main.js`
- `dist/robots.txt`
- `dist/sitemap.xml`
- copied image assets under `dist/assets/page-images/`

## Main Files

- `package.json` defines `build`, `lint`, and `dev`.
- `src/siteData.js` contains company details, navigation, solutions, proof points, case studies, industries, credibility signals, delivery steps, and capabilities.
- `src/styles.css` contains the refreshed visual system and responsive layout.
- `src/main.js` controls the mobile navigation toggle.
- `scripts/build.js` renders the six primary static pages, creates two legacy redirects, copies assets, writes `robots.txt`, and writes `sitemap.xml`.
- `scripts/lint.js` verifies required generated files, legacy redirects, banned phrases, HTML metadata, and semantic main landmarks.
- `scripts/server.js` serves `dist/` locally for development.

## Research Inspiration Used

The rebuild drew structure and messaging inspiration from leading field operations, SFA, and enterprise data sites. These were used for patterns only, not copied:

- Salesforce Field Service: cloud field operations, mobile workforce, dispatch, work order, and operations dashboard positioning.
- FieldAssist: FMCG sales and distribution structure, product grouping, industry segmentation, impact/proof flow, and demo-oriented CTA pattern.
- Esri ArcGIS Field Maps: mobile field data collection, maps, assets, location sharing, and inspection workflow framing.
- Repsly and Skynamo: field sales execution, retail execution, sales operations clarity, and conversion flow.
